# facial-recognition-ethics
Exploring the ethical implications of facial recognition technology
#`index.html` | What facial recognition is, how it works, an interactive false-match demo, and why we chose this topic |
#| `case-studies.html` | Two real-world examples: Apple Face ID and Rite Aid's in-store surveillance |
#| `ethics.html` | Social and political concerns, plus analysis through utilitarianism and rights-based ethics |
#| `recommendations.html` | Our technology, social, and policy recommendations, with an interactive "fix the system" tool |
