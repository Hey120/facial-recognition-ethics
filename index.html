01 / THE TECHNOLOGY

What is facial recognition?

Facial recognition is a type of biometric technology that analyzes facial features and compares them with enrolled data to verify or identify someone. It may use photographs, video frames, depth information, or infrared imagery, depending on the system.

01
▣

Capture

A camera captures an image or depth/infrared information of a face.

02
⌗

Represent

Software converts facial information into a numerical representation.

03
≈

Compare

The representation is compared with a stored template or a database.

04
✓

Decide

The system returns a match result or score, which may influence a human decision.

Technical references: Apple Face ID documentation ; NIST Face Recognition Technology Evaluation . This is a simplified general workflow, not a diagram of one specific product.

02 / WHY THIS MATTERS

The same capability. Different consequences.

A person choosing to unlock their own phone is not the same as a shopper being checked against a watchlist. The technology's purpose, consent model, accuracy, safeguards, and consequences all matter.
