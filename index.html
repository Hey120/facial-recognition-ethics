What Is Facial Recognition?

Facial recognition is a technology that uses computer algorithms to analyze and compare facial features to verify or identify individuals.

Why Does It Matter?

Facial recognition is used in phones, security systems, and other real-world settings. Although it offers convenience and security, it also raises concerns about privacy, accuracy, surveillance, and discrimination.
